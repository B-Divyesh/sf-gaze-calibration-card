How it works

Compare the pointer in three steps

Hardware calibration can be correct while today’s position has changed. This app checks the pointer you already use.

  1. 01

    Note the circumstances

    Optionally record posture, lenses, room light, or a monitor adjustment.

  2. 02

    Visit nine targets

    Your gaze-controlled system pointer is sampled locally while each target settles.

  3. 03

    Compare the pattern

    Review target error, drift direction, dwell reliability, and concrete next steps.

Privacy and limits

The app never requests camera access

It observes pointer positions from your existing gaze software. There is no login, cloud sync, advertising, analytics, or cursor control.

It does not diagnose a condition or replace your device maker’s calibration.
