Observe, mark, interpret

A setup check, not another calibration ritual.

Hardware calibration can be correct while today’s position is not. Gaze Calibration Card checks the pointer you already use, then gives you a plain-language signal before a frustrating session.

  1. 01

    Note the circumstances

    Optionally record posture, lenses, room light, or a monitor adjustment.

  2. 02

    Visit nine pollen marks

    Your gaze-controlled system pointer is sampled locally as each mark settles.

  3. 03

    Read the field note

    See target error, drift direction, dwell reliability, and concrete next steps.

A result you can act on

Reliable, borderline, or recalibrate first.

The support-friendly report includes thresholds and only the setup notes you approve. Save up to 50 checks locally, or keep no history at all.

Private by construction

Camera frames never enter the app.

The app observes only the pointer position your existing gaze software exposes to the operating system. There is no login, cloud sync, advertising, or analytics. Notes and checks stay in local app storage until you remove them.
