Terms of use

This free utility offers a practical indication of pointer reliability. It does not provide medical advice or certify a device.

Appropriate use

Use results as one signal when deciding whether to recalibrate or adjust a setup. Do not use the app for diagnosis, safety-critical control, or as a replacement for instructions from your eye-tracker manufacturer or clinician.

Device-dependent results

Scores depend on screen size, pointer settings, hardware, software drivers, posture, lighting, and the way the check is completed. Thresholds are transparent but are not universal clinical standards.
