Terms of use

This free utility compares pointer patterns. It does not provide medical advice or certify a device.

Appropriate use

Use results as one comparison when deciding whether to recalibrate or adjust a setup. Do not use the app for diagnosis, safety-critical control, or as a replacement for manufacturer or clinician instructions.

Device-dependent results

Scores depend on screen size, pointer settings, hardware, software drivers, posture, lighting, and check technique. The displayed pixel bands are not validated clinical standards.
